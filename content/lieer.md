</div>


## Privacy Policy {#privacy-policy}

This application accesses Gmail data through the Google Gmail API solely to synchronize the owner's mailbox with a local Maildir repository.

Email messages, labels, metadata and authentication credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer.

The application does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to third parties, use it for advertising, or use it to train machine learning models.

The owner can revoke the application's access at any time through their Google Account settings and delete the locally stored data.

This application is intended exclusively for personal use and is not offered as a service to other users.
